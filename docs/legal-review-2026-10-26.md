# rOndevu legal content review

The `/privacy` and `/tos` pages were revised on 26 October 2026 to describe behavior visible in the product without claiming certified KVKK or GDPR compliance. They are general product information and require operator and legal review before being treated as a formal privacy notice or final contractual terms.

## Facts still needed from the operator

- Legal identity and contact address of the entity operating `rondevu.org` and acting as data controller where applicable.
- Processing purpose and legal basis for each data category; collection method; recipient groups and cross-border transfers.
- Actual retention periods and deletion rules for accounts, bookings, technical logs, backups, and SMS records.
- Enabled production providers for SMS, email, calendars, video, analytics, hosting, and security, including their data locations.
- Whether special-category data may be entered into booking forms, and the controls and notices for that use.
- Commercial subscription, billing, cancellation, refund, dispute, and governing-law terms, if applicable.

The current privacy page must not be represented as a KVKK Article 10 notice until the required identity, purposes, recipients, collection method, legal bases, and data-subject rights are supplied and reviewed.
