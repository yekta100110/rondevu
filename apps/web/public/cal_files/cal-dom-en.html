Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

These are some of our most frequently asked questions.

These are some of our most frequently asked questions.

How do I handle emergencies or vacations?

+

Turning on time-off mode pauses your schedule, closes available slots, and optionally notifies affected clients.

Can I connect my own custom domain (e.g. drsmith.com)?

+

Yes, you can easily map your custom domain and run your booking page under your personal brand.

Why is SMS verification required for bookers?

+

A 6-digit code is sent to the client's phone before confirmation, preventing fake submissions and empty slots.

How does the calendar synchronization work?

+

Any personal event in your Google or Apple Calendar instantly blocks that time in rOndevu, eliminating conflicts.

Is there any commission or per-booking fee?

+
Yes. rOndevu is built to manage your appointments and availability securely. Your personal calendar notes remain private, and all data is encrypted and protected.
